Minnesota signed Tristan Leigh to its practice squad on Tuesday.

He hasn't spent time with any other NFL team this year. And he's still waiting to make his regular-season debut.

At 6-foot-4 and 312 pounds, the former Clemson tackle gives Minnesota another option to develop, and more depth at a position that suddenly matters a lot.

NFL.com's Lance Zierlein highlighted the former five-star recruit's length and athletic ability during the draft process. But he also explained why Leigh remained a project.

His evaluation was that Leigh could start a pass-protection rep with the right footwork and hand timing, and then struggle to anchor against power. Too much lunging could get him in trouble.

In the run game, Zierlein liked how quickly Leigh came out of his stance and reached his lateral landmarks, or got to defenders at the second level. The concern was whether he had enough sustained strength to finish those blocks.

So there are real movement skills to work with. But balance and handling power are the key parts of his development.
